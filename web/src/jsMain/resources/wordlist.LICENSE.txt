wordlist.txt is the BIP-39 English word list (2048 words, each unique in its first four letters), taken from the
MIT-licensed @scure/bip39 package (version 2.4.0), which carries it from the BIP-39 proposal. It is used here only to write
a 128-bit recovery value as 12 easy-to-copy words (ADR 0047); the words are not a wallet phrase (the checksum differs).
